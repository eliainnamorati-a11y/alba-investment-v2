Our Approach

Private-Equity style portfolio management meets a hedge-fund toolkit.

Like a private-equity investor, we hold a focused portfolio and study each position in depth. We use the full toolkit (long and short, cash and synthetic instruments, active duration management, relative-value switches) to express each view and control its risk. Proprietary data tools deliver real-time market intelligence; every decision remains human-led.

Diversified Concentration | Deep Due Diligence | Fundamental Value | Catalyst-Oriented | Active Duration | Relative Value | Instrument-Agnostic | Moderate Leverage

Our Flagship Strategy

Alba Credit Opportunities — A differentiated approach to global credit investing.

Alba Investment Partners’ first launch and flagship strategy, ACO offers continuity and an alternative to traditional credit investing. The fund focuses on performing credit, seeking to generate alpha from market dislocations and their event-driven normalisation. It takes a high-conviction approach, combined with strong risk management expressed through neutral credit exposure.
